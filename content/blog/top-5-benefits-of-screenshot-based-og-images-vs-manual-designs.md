When sharing a page on social media, the OG image is often the first thing people notice. Manual design can work, but automated screenshots offer several advantages.

## 1. Rapid setup

Creating graphics for every page takes hours. Screenshot-based OG images capture each page instantly, freeing up your design team.

## 2. Consistent branding

Screenshots always reflect your site's current styles, keeping fonts, colors, and layouts perfectly on-brand.

## 3. Effortless A/B testing

Launching a new variant? Automated screenshots generate matching OG images right away, so you can test pages without extra design work.

## 4. Fewer mistakes

Automation prevents typos and outdated visuals that slip into manually edited graphics.

## 5. Scales with your site

Once in place, screenshot generation creates OG images for any new page automatically.
