Juggling a handful of websites is no easy task. Between keeping content fresh and ensuring everything looks sharp on social media, it can quickly feel overwhelming.

That's where Mosaic steps in. By automatically generating Open Graph images from real page screenshots, Mosaic saves time you would otherwise spend designing images for each individual page.

You can integrate Mosaic into new projects in minutes, but the real magic happens once you have multiple sites under your belt. From a single dashboard, you can manage screenshots for all your domains, tweak settings, and automate updates whenever your pages change.

The result? Consistent, professional social previews across every project. Instead of manually capturing screenshots, you can get back to shipping features and growing your business.

Mosaic was built with busy founders in mind. If you maintain lots of sites or spin up new landing pages frequently, give Mosaic a try and see how much time you can save.
