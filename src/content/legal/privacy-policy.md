This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our services.

### Information We Collect

We collect:

- Account information (name, email address, sign-in details)
- The website hostnames you add to your account
- Screenshots of requested public pages (Cloudflare R2) and metadata such as page URL and generation time (Cloudflare D1)
- Billing information (payment method, billing address) for paid plans
- Usage data: hostname, outcome, and timing of each image request (Cloudflare Workers Analytics Engine)
- Technical information (IP address, device information, browser type) and request logs

### Use of Information

- To provide, maintain, and improve our services
- To process payments and fulfill orders
- To communicate with you about our services and updates
- To personalize your experience and provide relevant content
- To prevent abuse, including rate limiting with daily per-IP counts
- To comply with legal obligations and enforce our policies

### Data Security

- Encryption of data in transit and at rest
- Access controls and authentication mechanisms
- Regular security audits and vulnerability testing
- Secure data storage and backup procedures

### Third-Party Services

We may share your personal information with service providers that help operate our services, such as Clerk (authentication), Cloudflare (hosting, storage, screenshots, analytics), and payment processors.

### Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at [hello@praveenjuge.com](mailto:hello@praveenjuge.com).

### Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Screenshots are shared, viewable by anyone with the image URL, retaken when requested after 30 days, and kept when you remove a website.

### International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, with appropriate safeguards in place.
