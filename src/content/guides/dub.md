Custom link previews need a Dub Pro plan or higher.

1. Create or edit a link. Under **Custom Link Preview**, click the pencil on the preview (or press **L**).
2. Next to **Image**, click the link icon (**Paste a URL to an image**) and paste your destination page's encoded URL:

   ```text
   https://mosaic.praveenjuge.com/use?url=https%3A%2F%2Fyourwebsite.com%2Fyour_slug
   ```

3. Fill in the title and description, then click **Save changes**.

Dub keeps linking to the Mosaic URL instead of copying the image, so the preview stays up to date.
