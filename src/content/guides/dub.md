## Add a custom preview image in Dub

When creating a link, click **Edit** next to **Link Preview**, then use the image URL option with:

```text
https://mosaic.praveenjuge.com/use?url=https%3A%2F%2Fyourwebsite.com%2Fyour_slug&sig=SIGNATURE
```
