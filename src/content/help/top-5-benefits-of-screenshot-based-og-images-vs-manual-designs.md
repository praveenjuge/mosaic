The OG image is often the first thing people see when your page is shared.
Designing one by hand works, but screenshots have real advantages.

## 1. No design work

Making a graphic for every page takes hours. A screenshot is taken the first
time a page is shared.

## 2. Always on brand

A screenshot shows your real fonts, colors, and layout, so it matches your site.

## 3. Easy A/B testing

Each page variant at its own URL gets its own matching image, with no extra
design work.

## 4. Fewer mistakes

There's no separate graphic to keep in sync, so no typos or outdated visuals
slip in.

## 5. Scales with your site

New pages get an image automatically, and Mosaic retakes each one every 30 days.
