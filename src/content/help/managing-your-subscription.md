## Plans

Every plan includes the automatic shared OG image cache and unlimited websites.

- **Free** ($0): community forum support.
- **Pro** ($19/month): priority email support.
- **Pro Yearly** ($199/year): priority email support. Saves $29 a year compared
  to paying monthly.

Plans don't change how images work. Image refresh and the daily screenshot
limits are the same for everyone.

## Upgrade, change, or cancel

Mosaic doesn't have a billing page in the app. To upgrade, switch between
monthly and yearly, update payment details, get an invoice, or cancel, email
hello@praveenjuge.com from the address on your account.

You can cancel Pro at any time. You keep Pro support until the end of the
billing period you paid for.
