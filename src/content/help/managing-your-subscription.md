# Managing Your Subscription

Mosaic offers flexible subscription plans to meet your needs. Here's how to
manage your subscription.

## Available Plans

### Free Plan

- Automatic shared OG image cache
- Unlimited websites
- Community forum support

### Pro Plan ($19/month)

- Automatic shared OG image cache
- Unlimited websites
- Priority email support
- Advanced analytics

### Pro Yearly Plan ($199/year)

- Automatic shared OG image cache
- Unlimited websites
- Priority email support
- Advanced analytics
- Custom branding options
- Save $29 compared to monthly billing

## Upgrading Your Plan

1. Navigate to your **Settings** page
2. In the "Plan & Billing" section, click the **Upgrade** button for your
   desired plan
3. Complete the secure checkout process
4. Your plan will be activated immediately after payment

## Managing Your Subscription

Once you have a Pro plan, you can manage your subscription through the customer
portal:

1. Go to your **Settings** page
2. Click **Manage Subscription** on your current plan
3. This will open the customer portal where you can:
   - Update your payment method
   - Download invoices
   - Cancel your subscription

## Canceling Your Subscription

You can cancel your subscription at any time:

1. Access the customer portal (see above)
2. Click **Cancel Subscription**
3. You'll continue to have Pro access until the end of your current billing
   period

## Billing and Invoices

- All payments are processed securely through our payment provider
- Invoices are available in the customer portal
- Failed payments will result in service downgrade to the free plan
