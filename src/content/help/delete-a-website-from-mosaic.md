---
title: "Delete a Website from Mosaic"
description: "Remove a website from your account, and what happens to its images."
category: "Guides"
publishedAt: "2025-06-20T12:00:00.000Z"
---

1. Sign in and find the site in the **Websites** table on your dashboard.
2. Click the **⋯** button in the **Actions** column, then **Delete**.
3. Click **Yes, Delete** to confirm.

This removes the site from your account only. Cached images aren't deleted.

If no other account has that hostname saved, its `/use?url=` links return a 404
error. Remove them from your `og:image` tags first.

To change the hostname instead, choose **Edit** in the same menu.
