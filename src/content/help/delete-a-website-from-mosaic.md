---
title: "Delete a Website from Mosaic"
description: "Remove a saved website association from your Mosaic account."
category: "Guides"
publishedAt: "2025-06-20T12:00:00.000Z"
---

If you no longer want a website in your Mosaic account, you can remove its
association from your dashboard.

1. **Sign in to your Mosaic account** and open the **Websites** page.
2. **Choose the site** you want to remove and open its actions menu.
3. Click **Delete Website** and confirm the prompt.
4. The website is removed from your account. Shared cached images are not
   deleted and may still be used by other accounts that saved the hostname.

You can add the website again later if you change your mind.
