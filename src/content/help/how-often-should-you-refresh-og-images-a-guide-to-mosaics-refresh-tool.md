Mosaic keeps one screenshot per page URL and refreshes it automatically. You
can also regenerate a website's images yourself. Here's how that plays out.

## After a redesign

Your old screenshot stays in use until it's 30 days old. The next request after
that takes a new one with your new design. To skip the wait, choose
**Regenerate images** from the website's **…** menu on the dashboard. Every
image for the website is deleted, and each page gets a new screenshot on its
next request.

## For a launch

Regenerate the website's images once the new design is live. Social platforms
also keep their own copy of each preview. Their card debugger or re-scrape tool
makes them fetch the image Mosaic has now.

## For seasonal pages

Keep the page URL the same and let the 30-day refresh update it. Only use a new
URL when the page is truly new content. Every distinct URL, including different
query strings, gets its own screenshot.

## When Mosaic is busy

Mosaic has daily limits on new screenshots. If a refresh can't happen right
away, it keeps serving the older image, so your preview doesn't break.
